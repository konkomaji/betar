Privacy

The short version: Betar collects nothing. There is no server, no account, and no phone number, ever.

What Betar does not have

No account

No signup, no email, no phone number, no login.

No analytics

No usage tracking, no crash reports sent anywhere.

No advertising

Nothing to sell, nothing to target.

No server logs

There is no server, so there is nothing keeping a record of who you talked to or what you posted.

What is stored, and where

A device identity and its keys are generated on first launch and kept only on the device, encrypted at rest. Direct messages, groups and channels are end-to-end encrypted before they ever leave your phone. A public feed post is, by its nature, meant to be seen: it travels the mesh and, once a relay bridge is reached, the open Nostr relay network, same as any other post on that network. Nothing you mark private is ever uploaded anywhere, because there is no server for it to go to.

Location: one tap, one time

Betar never shares your location unless you tap the share button inside a specific chat. That single tap sends your location once, to that conversation only. There is no continuous or background location sharing anywhere in the app.

What nearby phones and relays can and cannot see

A nearby phone can see that your device is present and can pass along messages addressed through it, the same way any phone in the mesh does. It cannot read a direct message, group message, or channel message that is not addressed to it: those are encrypted for their recipient before they ever leave your phone. A relay bridging your feed posts to the internet only ever sees what a feed post already is in public: a post.

Permissions, and why

Betar asks for Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Direct permissions to find and talk to nearby phones, location permission only where the operating system requires it for that same nearby discovery, not to track where you are, and internet access so your device can bridge to the open Nostr relay network when you choose a public feed room that needs it. It does not ask for contacts or anything unrelated to those two jobs.

Children

Betar does not collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information at all.

How to check this yourself

Every claim on this page is verifiable in the source code, which anyone can read, build, and check against what the app actually does.
