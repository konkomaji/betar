Why Betar exists

A platform that depends on a server can be subpoenaed, throttled, or shut off. Betar has none, so there is nothing to subpoena, throttle, or shut off. This is not hypothetical.

Iran, 2026

An 88-day nationwide internet shutdown, the longest on record, cutting off tens of millions of people from each other and the outside world.

France, October 2026

The prime minister asked the country's four mobile carriers to be ready to block four social platforms for several hours.

India, July 2026

GitHub was ordered to restrict access to three repositories of Bitchat, the app that inspired Betar, over its anonymous, decentralized design.

How it works

Your phone talks directly to nearby phones over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Direct. No internet needed for that. Those phones relay for each other too, so a message can reach someone far outside your own radio range over time, carried hop to hop, the same way a rumor spreads through a crowd. The moment any one device anywhere in that chain reaches the internet, even briefly, it bridges everything to the open Nostr relay network and back. You never need your own internet connection, only someone near you, eventually, does.

Local mesh

Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Direct, phone to phone, zero infrastructure, works the moment two phones are near each other.

Relay bridge

Any device with internet bridges the local mesh to the public Nostr relay network. Kill one relay, another carries on.

Global reach

The same content, synced wherever the network can carry it, with no single company or server deciding who gets to see it.

What's inside

Betar is a feed and a messenger in one app, built end to end on the idea that nobody should be able to take either away from you.

Feed

Posts live in location-scoped rooms, block to country precision, not one global timeline. Reverse-chronological only, nothing ranked by an algorithm.

Chat

Direct messages, groups, and passphrase-protected channels, all end-to-end encrypted: Noise, X3DH, post-quantum PQXDH for first contact, MLS for groups.

Community Notes

Anyone can attach a note to a post, backed by two source links and a short explanation. A public scoring method decides what surfaces, not a moderator.

Safety

No phone number, no email, ever. A short ID fingerprinted from your own key. One-shot location sharing only, never continuous tracking. Panic wipe when you need it.

Built on an open protocol

Betar is the reference application. The protocol, the cryptography, and the Rust core live in Project Mesh, 191 passing tests, fully open source, so any other app can build on the same canonical core instead of a fork of this one. Read the whitepaper or the threat model for what this does and does not claim. No independent security audit has happened yet.

Frequently asked

What is Betar?

Betar is a social and chat app built so that no government, company or platform operator can ban an account or remove a post, because there is no server to pressure. It carries a public feed, direct messages, groups, channels, and a crowd-sourced fact-check layer called Community Notes.

Does Betar need an internet connection?

Not for people near you. Phones talk directly over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Direct and relay for each other. The moment any one nearby device reaches the internet, even briefly, it bridges local content to the open Nostr relay network and back, so reach is not capped by radio range.

Does Betar require an account, phone number or login?

No. There is no signup, no account, no email, no phone number and no login. Your identity is a keypair generated on your own device, with a short ID that is a fingerprint of that key.

Is Betar open source?

Yes. Betar is built on Project Mesh, an open protocol and Rust core released under the AGPL-3.0 license. Anyone may read, build and check the source.

Why isn't Betar on Google Play or the App Store first?

App stores are themselves a chokepoint. Bitchat, the app that inspired Betar, was pulled from Google Play and the App Store in India on 2026-10-03, and from Apple's China App Store in April 2026. Betar ships on F-Droid and as a direct APK download first, so no single store can make it disappear.

Betar has not had an independent security review yet. Read safety and limits before relying on it for anything where a real vulnerability would put you at risk.
